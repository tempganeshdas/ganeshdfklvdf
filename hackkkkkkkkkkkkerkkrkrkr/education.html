What Causes Climate Change?

Understanding the primary drivers of global warming and climate disruption

🏭

Burning Fossil Fuels

Coal, oil, and natural gas combustion releases massive amounts of CO₂ and other greenhouse gases into the atmosphere.

Click to learn more ↓

The Science Behind It

  • • Fossil fuels store carbon that was trapped underground for millions of years
  • • Burning releases this carbon as CO₂, CO, and other greenhouse gases
  • • Transportation, electricity generation, and industry are major sources
  • • Accounts for approximately 75% of global greenhouse gas emissions

Impact: Global CO₂ levels have increased by over 40% since pre-industrial times

🌳

Deforestation

Forests act as carbon sinks, but widespread clearing reduces Earth's capacity to absorb CO₂ from the atmosphere.

Click to learn more ↓

Forest Loss Impact

  • • Forests absorb ~2.4 billion tons of CO₂ annually
  • • Amazon rainforest alone stores 150-200 billion tons of carbon
  • • Agriculture, logging, and urban expansion drive deforestation
  • • Reduces biodiversity and disrupts local climate patterns

Scale: 10 million hectares of forest lost annually (equivalent to Iceland's area)

🏗️

Industrial Emissions

Manufacturing processes release greenhouse gases directly and consume large amounts of fossil fuel-based energy.

Click to learn more ↓

Industrial Processes

  • • Cement production alone accounts for 8% of global CO₂ emissions
  • • Steel, chemicals, and manufacturing are major contributors
  • • Process emissions + energy consumption = total industrial footprint
  • • Many industrial processes release non-CO₂ greenhouse gases

Global Share: Industry contributes ~21% of total greenhouse gas emissions

🐄

Agriculture & Livestock

Farming produces methane from livestock and rice cultivation, plus nitrous oxide from fertilizers.

Click to learn more ↓

Agricultural Emissions

  • • Methane from livestock digestion (enteric fermentation)
  • • Methane from rice paddies and manure management
  • • Nitrous oxide from synthetic fertilizers and soil management
  • • CO₂ from agricultural machinery and land use changes

Methane Facts: Agriculture produces ~40% of human-caused methane emissions

🗑️

Waste Management

Landfills produce methane as organic waste decomposes, while incineration releases CO₂ and other pollutants.

Click to learn more ↓

Waste Impact

  • • Landfills are the 3rd largest source of human-related methane emissions
  • • Organic waste decomposition produces methane in anaerobic conditions
  • • Incineration releases CO₂, particulates, and toxic substances
  • • Inadequate recycling increases the need for virgin material extraction

Global Impact: Waste sector accounts for ~3% of global greenhouse gas emissions

🚗

Transportation

Cars, trucks, ships, and planes burn fossil fuels, making transportation a major source of greenhouse gas emissions.

Click to learn more ↓

Transportation Emissions

  • • Road vehicles account for ~72% of transport emissions
  • • Aviation contributes ~11% of transport emissions
  • • Shipping produces ~14% of transport emissions
  • • Transportation is the fastest-growing source of emissions globally

Global Share: Transportation contributes ~16% of total greenhouse gas emissions

Effects of Climate Change

The real-world impacts of climate change that we're already experiencing

🌡️

Rising Temperatures

Global average temperature increase

Since 1880 +1.2°C

Global temperatures have risen by approximately 1.2°C since pre-industrial times, with most warming occurring in the past 40 years.

🌊

Sea Level Rise

Coastal flooding and erosion

Since 1900 +21cm

Sea levels are rising at an accelerating rate, threatening coastal communities and low-lying areas worldwide.

🌪️

Extreme Weather

More frequent and intense events

Hurricane intensity +25%

Heat waves, droughts, floods, and storms are becoming more frequent and severe in many regions.

🦋

Biodiversity Loss

Species extinction and habitat loss

Species at risk 1M+

Climate change is driving species extinction at rates 100-1,000 times faster than natural background rates.

Climate Solutions

Proven strategies and technologies to reduce emissions and build climate resilience

☀️

Renewable Energy Transition

Solar, wind, hydro, and other renewable sources can provide clean electricity without greenhouse gas emissions.

Solar costs have fallen 89% since 2010
Wind energy is now the cheapest source of power in many regions
Could supply 90% of electricity needs by 2050
💡

Energy Efficiency

Using less energy to accomplish the same tasks reduces emissions while saving money and resources.

LED lighting uses 75% less energy than incandescent bulbs
Smart thermostats can reduce heating/cooling by 10-15%
Building insulation can cut energy use by 30-50%
🚊

Sustainable Transportation

Electric vehicles, public transit, cycling, and walking can dramatically reduce transportation emissions.

EVs produce 60-70% fewer emissions than gas cars
Public transit reduces per-capita emissions by 45%
Cycling and walking produce zero emissions
🌱

Nature-Based Solutions

Forests, wetlands, and oceans naturally absorb CO₂ while providing multiple environmental benefits.

Forests absorb 2.4 billion tons of CO₂ annually
Wetlands store 30% of global soil carbon
Reforestation could remove 5-10 Gt CO₂ per year
